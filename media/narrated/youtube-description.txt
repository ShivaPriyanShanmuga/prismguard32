PrismGuard-32: a narrated research demonstration for the Cryptographer track of Mathematics for Systems Security.

Source, mathematical paper, and reproducible evidence:
https://github.com/ShivaPriyanShanmuga/prismguard32

The Rust prototype wraps a synthetic 32-byte master key in an authenticated capsule. Private arithmetic stays in randomized shares; verification releases its final decision; the receiver supports bounded recovery after hostile packets.

The walkthrough includes actual captured demonstration output, exact small-field experiments, correlated-share and squaring checks, and deliberately leaky negative controls. It uses caption cards and a captured terminal excerpt rather than a continuous screen recording.

Research limits: fresh one-use material and trusted provisioning are required. The arithmetic model is not a physical side-channel certificate or an independent cryptographic audit. No real keys or user files are used.
